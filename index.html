intonIQ Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 30, 2026

No data collection

intonIQ does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. All recordings, transcriptions and AI analysis are processed entirely on your device.

Microphone

Microphone access is used solely for recording audio sessions. Audio is never uploaded to any server.

iCloud

Sessions can optionally sync via your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This data is only accessible to you and governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Apple Intelligence

AI analysis is performed on-device using Apple Intelligence. No text or audio is sent to external servers.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple's App Store. intonIQ does not process or store any payment information.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@intoniq.se
